# LegalVC-OPC Operating System

> **Investing in Legal Experience.**  
> **Engineering Legal Experience into AI-native Value.**

Version: 1.0  
Status: Operating framework for internal execution and project validation  
Owner: Human Organizer / LegalVC-OPC  
Pilot: 4C AI-native Equity Intelligence

## 1. Purpose

LegalVC-OPC Operating System（LegalVC-OS）是法务VC的内部生产与投资执行系统。它把 Legal Experience Investment Methodology 转化为可持续执行的工作流程、职责、档案、质量门槛、投资记录和项目复盘机制。

系统的核心任务不是管理一般法律行业项目，而是持续完成：

**发现法律专业经验 → 判断经验价值 → 确认来源与权利 → 经验工程化 → AI-native 产品化 → 客户使用与收入验证 → 收益分配与再投资。**

法律专业经验是核心投资对象。AI-native 产品是优先商业化路径。项目、公司、软件、Agent、专家合作和资金安排是价值实现方式、工程载体或资源投入，不取代核心投资对象。

## 2. Operating principles

1. **Experience-first：** 每个机会必须明确指出所研究的法律专业经验，以及经验对应的具体任务和判断。
2. **Customer-task-first：** 产品从客户任务和结果定义开始，不从模型、Agent 或 Demo 开始。
3. **Rights-before-use：** 对经验材料、客户资料、案例、模型、品牌和衍生成果逐项确认来源与使用权利。没有合法基础和必要授权，不进入相关数据处理或产品化。
4. **Evidence-led：** 每项关键判断记录证据来源、日期、事实/陈述/假设类别、反证和未解决问题。
5. **Engineering with evaluation：** 经验资产、工作流和产品能力必须可测试、可追溯、可版本化。
6. **AI-native by design：** 对可工程化的经验，优先让产品直接完成标准任务；专家处理经验贡献、复杂判断、审核和例外，不默认逐单人工交付。
7. **Deterministic where needed：** 计算、规则校验和重要数据变换使用可审计程序；不能把精确计算交给自由生成。
8. **Customer data is not training data by default：** 客户数据不得自动进入训练、跨客户知识库或经验资产。
9. **Stage-gated capital：** 资金按阶段释放；未满足门槛，不因为已经投入时间或代码而自动追加。
10. **Human accountability：** Agent 可辅助，不拥有独立授权、法律人格、投资决策权或对外承诺权。

## 3. Two connected loops

### 3.1 Experience Investment Loop

Discover → Evaluate → Rights → Engineer → AI-native Productize → Customer Usage & Revenue → Return Allocation → Reinvest.

用于回答：投资什么经验、为什么投入、取得什么权利、如何商业化、谁取得什么经济利益。

### 3.2 Product Learning Loop

Customer Task → Agent Execution → Evaluation → Professional Correction → Authorized Feedback → Experience Asset / Product Update.

用于回答：产品如何在真实任务中验证和提高专业能力、质量、复用程度及单位经济性。

两条闭环相连但不能混为一条。客户使用数据和反馈只有在具有合法基础、明确授权、用途限制、必要脱敏和专业审核后，才可用于经验资产更新。

## 4. Operating model

LegalVC-OS 采用一个 Human Organizer、按任务组合的 Agent Team、外部专业网络和项目化投资治理。

- **Human Organizer：** 唯一的运行总负责人；负责投资主题、项目优先级、关键判断、资源配置、权利升级、阶段决策和对外责任。
- **Agent Team：** 执行资料搜集、分析初稿、档案结构化、工程任务、测试和指标计算。
- **External Professional Network：** 提供可授权经验、专业复核、客户场景、技术协作和商业渠道。
- **Project Record：** 每个项目必须有统一项目编号、Experience Profile、Rights Matrix、Evaluation Record、Investment Memo 和阶段决策记录。

Agent 不能自行确认权利归属、代表律师发表专业法律意见、签署合同、批准支出或作出投资承诺。

## 5. Lifecycle and stage gates

| Stage | Purpose | Required exit artifact |
|---|---|---|
| L0 Opportunity | 发现有具体任务场景的经验机会 | Opportunity Record |
| L1 Experience Profile | 识别经验、判断与适用边界 | Experience Profile |
| L2 Value & Demand | 评估专业价值和客户需求 | Value / Demand Assessment |
| L3 Rights & Provenance | 核验来源、主体、授权和资料用途 | Rights & Provenance Matrix |
| L4 Engineering | 经验结构化、AI 能力构建和测试 | Experience Asset Pack + Eval Report |
| L5 Product & Customer | 客户任务、产品质量、使用和付费验证 | Product Evaluation + Pilot Report |
| L6 Investment & Return | 形成项目投入、权利、收入、回报和复投决策 | Investment Memo + Gate Decision |

阶段允许回退、暂停或终止。权利门槛、重大专业质量门槛或客户需求门槛不能由其他维度高分抵消。

## 6. Canonical records

每个项目使用以下记录作为事实来源（single source of truth）：

- Opportunity Register：所有机会及当前阶段。
- Experience Profile：经验是什么、来自哪里、如何发挥专业判断。
- Rights & Provenance Matrix：来源、权利主体、授权用途和限制。
- Experience Asset Register：知识模型、规则、Skills、案例和测试集的版本。
- Product Evaluation Record：任务表现、重大错误、人工接管、成本和版本。
- Pilot Report：客户使用、付款、反馈和交付结果。
- Investment Memo：投资命题、投入、可取得权益、里程碑、停止条件。
- Gate Decision Log：谁在何时批准、暂停、要求补证或终止。

仓库只保存经批准可进入代码仓库的非敏感材料、模板、合成样本和决策元数据。客户资料、案件材料、个人信息、商业秘密、密码、访问令牌和未经授权的文件不得提交到 Git 仓库。

## 7. Decision rights

| Decision | Agent | Human Organizer | External professional / authorized entity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opportunity discovery | Prepare | Approve priority | Contribute leads |
| Experience interpretation | Draft and organize | Review | Validate professional content |
| Rights conclusion | Gather evidence only | Coordinate escalation | Qualified legal review / rights holder confirmation |
| Engineering and testing | Execute scoped tasks | Accept deliverables | Professional evaluator validates |
| Product release | Test and recommend | Approve internal release | Authorized professional signs off on regulated/high-risk work |
| Budget and investment | Analyze only | Recommend / approve within authority | Legal entity / authorized investment body approves |
| Contract and legal service | No authority | Coordinate only | Appropriate legal entity and qualified professionals |

## 8. Weekly operating cadence

- **Monday — Portfolio triage:** Review opportunity register, stage, owner, blocker and next decision.
- **Tuesday — Experience research:** Interviews, case reconstruction, task and judgment extraction.
- **Wednesday — Rights and asset review:** Source, authorization, data and experience asset review.
- **Thursday — Engineering and evaluation:** Build or revise assets, tests, product workflows and cost measurements.
- **Friday — Customer and investment review:** Usage, payment, economics, gate decisions, resource allocation and retrospective.

A weekly cycle does not require every project to move forward. A documented pause or stop is a valid operating outcome.

## 10. Implementation policy

Version 1.0 is document-driven. Do not build an internal SaaS workbench before the process has been exercised on 4C and at least one additional experience opportunity. After the records, fields and stage gates prove stable, automate the repeated parts.

**The first proof of LegalVC-OS is not the existence of these files. It is the successful execution of Pilot 01 with traceable experience rights, tested AI-native capability, real customer usage, payment evidence and an auditable investment decision.**
