## 2. Distinguish historical facts from new research

The 2018 interview is a public historical source. It may be used as a citation and research lead, subject to normal copyright and source-use limits. It does not by itself grant rights to:
- the original 4C formula or non-public method;
- source databases, working papers or case materials;
- customer / company files;
- brand, logo or endorsement;
- templates, software or unpublished materials;
- derivative product commercialization.

Do not copy protected or confidential content into the product or repository based solely on the press article.

## 3. Asset status vocabulary

- DISCOVERED: potential asset identified.
- SOURCED: source and contributor identified.
- RIGHTS_PENDING: use rights not yet confirmed.
- LIMITED_USE: specific, documented uses approved.
- ENGINEERED: structured as knowledge / rules / workflow.
- VALIDATED: reviewed and tested by qualified evaluators.
- PRODUCTIZED: embedded in a release-approved product.
- RETIRED: withdrawn, superseded or no longer valid.
