## G0 — Discovery and rights

Deliverables:
- Experience Profile v0.1;
- Rights & Provenance Matrix v0.1;
- 5–8 founder / buyer interviews planned or completed;
- 2–3 professional evaluator interviews planned or completed;
- first customer task definition;
- rights and clean-room options;
- updated risk and evidence register.

Stop / hold:
- no authority to use original 4C material;
- no expert access or cooperation path;
- no evidence of a meaningful customer problem after discovery;
- no safe basis to collect required information.

## G1 — Experience engineering

Deliverables:
- authorized experience source package or independently developed clean-room asset base;
- expert-reviewed role / input / judgment / output model;
- explicit assumptions and exception taxonomy;
- calculation specification;
- evaluation cases and scoring rubric;
- product requirements and prototype acceptance criteria.

Stop / hold:
- key judgments cannot be expressed or appropriately escalated;
- rights limits prevent required product use;
- no reliable quality baseline can be created.

## G2 — Codex-first MVP

Deliverables:
- guided intake;
- missing-data and inconsistency handling;
- scenario comparison;
- deterministic calculation engine;
- rationale and assumptions report;
- professional review handoff;
- audit, access and deletion controls;
- test and build evidence.

Stop / hold:
- critical tests fail;
- calculation integrity cannot be established;
- unacceptable data exposure / security issue;
- human intervention means the product does not materially complete the intended task.

## G3 — Customer and commercial validation

Deliverables:
- 1–2 controlled pilot reports;
- expert evaluation;
- customer usage and task-completion data;
- payment / contract evidence;
- per-task cost and human-review analysis;
- revised business and investment memo.

Scale only if professional quality, customer value, usage and plausible unit economics are jointly supported.

## Investment decision vocabulary

- PASS: proceed to next gate.
- CONDITIONAL PASS: proceed only after specified conditions are met.
- HOLD: no new spend beyond safe preservation / evidence work.
- STOP: cease the path; preserve only permitted records and execute data / asset disposition.

## First approval requested

The only initial approval request is G0, capped at ¥40,000. This gate decision does not approve G1–G3 or the reserve. Spending must be approved by the legally authorized entity / person under the applicable governance and finance rules.
