# Pilot 01 — 4C AI-native Equity Intelligence

> Working title only. External use of the 4C name, model, brand, source materials or historical cases is subject to rights and authorization review.

Project ID: LV-2026-001  
Status: L0 / Conditional Pass  
Owner: Human Organizer  
Investment thesis: Legal Experience → AI-native Equity Intelligence

## 1. Why this project

The 2018 法务VC 4C 股权法律服务实践 is the first historical case selected to test the LegalVC-OPC Operating System. It provides a documented historical path in which equity-related professional experience was articulated through a dynamic equity model and delivered through traditional advisory and legal-service products.

The new project is not a continuation of the old service business and not a simple software wrapper around human consulting. It is a new investment hypothesis: re-identify the underlying equity-design legal experience, verify its source and use rights, engineer it into testable experience assets, and develop an AI-native product that lets founders directly complete a defined equity-design task.

**The historical 4C model and the new AI-native product are not assumed to be the same asset, nor is any license, ownership or affiliation presumed.**

## 2. Core experience hypothesis

Candidate experience domains to investigate:
- identifying founder, co-founder, core employee and investor roles;
- structuring contribution, cash / non-cash input and future commitment information;
- reasoning about initial equity allocation and incentive pools;
- explaining control and governance implications;
- modeling financing dilution and future changes;
- identifying departures, vesting, buyback and dispute scenarios;
- recognizing when a standard model is not suitable and professional escalation is required.

These are research hypotheses derived from the historical case context, not verified rules or an assertion that the historical model contains each capability.

## 3. First customer task

**A founding team wants to explore and compare initial equity-structure scenarios using structured information about team roles, contributions, commitments and plans.**

The first release should help users:
1. provide structured team and company information;
2. answer necessary follow-up questions;
3. view several transparent allocation scenarios;
4. inspect dilution and pool calculations;
5. understand assumptions, trade-offs and unresolved issues;
6. export a structured discussion report for review.

The product should not claim to autonomously provide a final legal opinion, choose the legally correct allocation, execute a cap-table change or replace qualified professional review.

## 4. Target users and buyers

Initial hypotheses:
- users: startup founders and co-founder teams;
- buyers: founders, incubators, accelerators or startup service providers;
- validators: experienced equity lawyers, corporate counsel and other qualified professionals;
- initial channel partners: entrepreneurship support organizations and professional networks.

Validate the actual buyer, budget owner and purchasing process before settling pricing.

## 5. Product principle

**AI-native by task, not by label.** The system must directly complete an end-to-end customer task with controlled quality. It is not successful merely because it has a chat interface or generates a polished report.

Use:
- conversational intake and missing-information detection;
- explicit experience assets and rule provenance;
- deterministic, testable computation for shares and dilution;
- LLM-assisted explanation and document drafting within a defined scope;
- professional review and escalation for legal judgment, special structures or high-risk events.

## 6. Current gate and permitted work

L0 is conditionally passed for discovery only.

Permitted:
- research using public, lawfully accessible information;
- interviews without requesting confidential matter files;
- synthetic scenario design;
- customer problem and willingness-to-pay discovery;
- mapping historical claims to open questions.

Not yet permitted:
- using original 4C materials, non-public models, source documents or customer cases without documented authorization;
- using 法务VC / 4C brand assets in external commercial product claims without rights clearance;
- training or indexing customer / client materials;
- presenting historical media claims as audited current performance;
- publicly launching an AI product as the licensed or official 4C product.

## 8. Success criteria

For the first pilot:
- source and authority path documented;
- a bounded, expert-reviewed Experience Asset Pack exists;
- product completes the agreed founding-team equity exploration workflow;
- deterministic calculations pass a professional test suite;
- critical failure modes and escalation rules are documented;
- 1–2 real pilot customers complete the workflow;
- at least one paid engagement is sought as a minimum commercial signal; a stronger target is two paid engagements;
- model, tool, human-review, support and acquisition costs are measured;
- contributor rights and product economics are contractually defined.

The pilot is not deemed successful solely because the MVP ships.
